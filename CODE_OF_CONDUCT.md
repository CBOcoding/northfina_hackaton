# Code of Conduct

This hackathon is for learning, building and meeting people. Everyone should feel welcome, whatever their background, experience, gender, identity, origin or language.

**Do:**

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to something;
- give credit, including to the tools and libraries you use;
- ask for help early, and help others when you can.

**Don't:**

- harass, insult or exclude anyone, in person, on Discord or on GitHub;
- copy another team's work, or sabotage it;
- share other people's personal data, or put secrets in a repository.

If something feels wrong, tell a Northfina organizer or Hive staff on site, or send an `Organizer` a direct message on Discord. We'll handle it confidentially. Organizers may warn, remove or disqualify anyone who breaks these rules.

Hive Helsinki's own house rules apply in the building.
